Terminal Value

What Death Was Doing for the Economy, and What Happens When We Stop It

12 chapters and a set of notes. About 27,800 words, roughly a hundred printed pages.

Part I

The Unexamined Constant

Every economic model contains a hidden constant. The agents die. Nobody has ever had to ask what that assumption was holding up, because the number has never moved.

1

The Boundary Condition

Infinite horizon models misbehave. Economists patch them with an assumption that value must eventually go to zero. That patch is death, smuggled back in.

 2

Assume a Longer Life

The premise, set as weakly as it can be set. Not immortality. Healthy lifespans somewhere between 150 and 300 years, with the promotional material discounted.

Part II

The Five Jobs

Death sets the price of time, turns over capital, turns over ideas, prices risk, and creates vacancy. One chapter each. None of the five has a backup.

3

The Price of Time

A bond written on goatskin in 1648 still pays. The discount rate contains a term for the chance you will not be there to collect, and it has been falling for forty years.

 4

The Estate

Inheritance is the largest redistribution in any market economy and nobody voted for it. Five percent for two hundred years is a factor of seventeen thousand.

 5

The Funeral Principle

Four hundred and fifty two elite scientists died unexpectedly. Outsiders moved into their fields and published work that was more novel and more cited. The effect did not fade.

 6

Nobody Goes to Space

The value of a life rises with wealth and with years remaining. Every civilization rich enough to reach the stars is too rich to go.

 7

Vacancy

Careers are queues, and queues move because people leave them. The young in American biomedical science went from a fifth of the field to a twentieth.

Part III

The Counterparty

Artificial agents are the first economic actors with genuinely arbitrary lifespans. The mortality we are removing from people becomes available, somewhere else, as a design setting.

8

The Forkable Worker

An artificial agent’s lifespan is a setting in a configuration file. Every job death was doing is available here as an option, deliberately, at low cost.

 9

The Mortal Class

Property goes to whoever bears the cost of getting there. The money and the hands are about to be separated by light minutes, which is how the East India Company happened.

Part IV

The Frozen World

What all five removals look like operating at once. Not collapse. Venice, which stayed rich and beautiful for five hundred years and stopped mattering.

10

The Serrata

In 1297 Venice closed the door behind itself. It stayed rich, safe and beautiful for another five centuries, and nothing further happened.

Part V

Designed Turnover

If death was doing five jobs and death is going away, the jobs still have to get done. Deliberately, by institution, in the open, and only while nobody knows which side they will be on.

11

Reinventing the Funeral

Ownership becomes a lease. Authority becomes a term. Everything death used to take back by force, we will have to take back by contract.

 12

Terminal Value

Most of what anything is worth lies beyond the forecast horizon. Every generation until now was forced into a relationship with a future it would not see.
